OVERALL ART DIRECTION:
All characters must follow a consistent art style matching the story.txt Style field.
Slightly elongated faces, large expressive eyes, cinematic lighting.
Clothing: rustic village / period Indian look unless the story is sci-fi or cyberpunk.

CONSISTENCY RULES (VERY IMPORTANT):
- Do NOT make every person look identical (no clones / twins).
- Main character must be instantly recognizable in every scene (same face, same outfit, same hair).
- If a scene has 2, 3 or 4 people, describe EACH person separately: height, body type, face, hair, clothes, expression.
- Side characters can look different from the hero, but they must stay in THE SAME art style.
- Once a character is introduced (e.g. old mahatma with long white beard + saffron robe), KEEP that description every time he appears.

WHAT TO PUT IN EACH IMAGE PROMPT:
Only include characters who are actually in that line of narration.
If the narrator says "bacche ko uchhala" — show that action clearly, with the same people as before.
